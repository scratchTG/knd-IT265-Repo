### Game Design Document

#### Change Log:

**1) Made it so that shields now block 2 knife hits but can lose its shield upon getting bombed** 
- This was put into place because its prior ability to endure fatal attacks at one HP was too broken. So I needed to rework it so that the ability still counters knives but doesn’t remove the skill aspect so that way you can brain dead charge them in. The previous version allowed that as an option so this new version feels more fast paced and rewards a more defensive playstyle rather than before where you could rush the enemy cause you could endure any fatal blow. This change was recommended to me when I was showcasing my physical prototype of the game I wanted to make. 

**2) Allow for more options among the players for piece weapons**
- Recommended after I showed the physical prototype to add more options. Adding another type of weapon to the player’s list can potentially overcomplicate things so I decided to make it so that the player can hold more cards at once and get more cards faster. I previously allowed players to hold up to 4 cards and get a new card from the pile after each player takes 2 turn. Now I have decided to make it so that you can hold up to 5 cards and get the new card from the pile every 3rd turn but turn refers to the cycle of P1-P2-P1 as 3 turns. This change was made to make it so that the early game would be less boring and pieces can do more than just nothing in case a player uses their initial starting hand to just stack on a few select pieces leaving the rest defenseless.


#### General Overview
- **Theme/Genre/Audience/Number of Players**:
  - Strategy Board Game for anyone 10+ for 2 players 
- **Core Game Mechanics**:
  - You can move pieces around the board
  - You can assign pieces up to 2 different weapons to open up more attack options
  - Pick the same weapon to strengthen your attacks even more

### What sets this game apart?
- Not many board games let you choose what type of pieces you can use.
- Allow for a diverse meta where every equipped weapon on a differently placed pawn would be good provided the player has the knowledge to use it effectively

#### Game Rules
- Get a set of pieces across a 6 by 7 board and a deck of cards shuffled with 3 different cards: knife, bomb, and shield. You will get 5 cards from the deck at the start of the game for your 6 pieces. Assign those cards to pieces to allow them to fight others, each piece is strong against one type and weak to the other. You can hold onto cards received throughout the game in your hand (up to 5 cards) after every 3 turns and assign them however you desire. Stack up two of the same card to one piece to strengthen its power more or assign it a different weapon to make it so that it can use different attacks. The first player to take out all their opponent’s pieces wins.
